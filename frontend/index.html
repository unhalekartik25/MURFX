Four Pillars of SPHEREx Infrared Exploration

MURFX bridges cutting-edge NASA astrophysics with high-throughput citizen science, turning complex multi-epoch spectral cubes into effortless celestial discoveries.

PILLAR 01 TEMPORAL & SPECTRAL

Dual-Mode Time Video & Dipole Arithmetic

Toggle between Time Movie Mode (fixed wavelength across 6-month survey passes) and Spectral Movie Mode. Instant Diff (B−A) subtraction erases static stars and isolates moving objects as unmistakable black/white dipoles, boosted by 6-array SNR co-addition.

PILLAR 02 MOLECULAR PROFILES

On-the-Fly 102-Channel Molecular Spectroscopy

Every pixel carries a full 102-band spectrum (0.75–5.00 μm). Instantly diagnose brown dwarf atmospheres, volatile comet outgassing, and icy interstellar grains with shaded absorption band markers.

H2O (1.4 μm) CH4/H2 (2.3 μm) CO2 (4.26 μm) CO Peak (4.7 μm)
PILLAR 03 ASTROMETRIC RIGOR

Epoch Propagation & Sub-Pixel Centroiding

Solves the J2000 stale-epoch problem: MURFX propagates catalog proper motion to exact observation frame MJD and calculates a 5×5 flux-weighted sub-pixel centroid, synchronized with CDS Aladin Lite, SIMBAD, and AllWISE overlays.

SIMULTANEOUS MULTI-SPECTRAL IMAGING

Dichroic Beamsplitter Optical Architecture

SPHEREx divides incoming starlight into three optical channels via dichroic beamsplitters, exposing two distinct detector arrays simultaneously. Click a pair to inspect its diagnostic spectral capabilities:

PAIR 1 // OPTICAL TO THERMAL BEAMSPLITTER 1
Band D1 0.75 – 1.11 μm R = 41
Band D4 2.42 – 3.82 μm R = 35

Balances near-IR continuum with the 3.0 μm water frost window. Crucial for detecting cool M/L dwarfs and differentiating asteroids from background galaxies.

PAIR 2 // CARBON DIOXIDE ICE BEAMSPLITTER 2
Band D2 1.11 – 1.64 μm R = 41
Band D5 3.82 – 4.42 μm R = 110 • CO2

High-resolution R=110 channel dedicated to the 4.26 μm Carbon Dioxide (CO2) ice/gas absorption band in outer solar system comets and cool planetary atmospheres.

PAIR 3 // THERMAL Y-DWARFS BEAMSPLITTER 3
Band D3 1.64 – 2.42 μm R = 41
Band D6 4.42 – 5.00 μm R = 130 • CO/Y-Dwarf

Highest resolving power (R=130) capturing the peak 4.5–5.0 μm thermal atmospheric emission of ultra-cool sub-stellar worlds (~250 K / −23 °C).

WISE 0855: Sub-stellar Brown Dwarf

WISE 0855 is the fourth-closest known system to the Sun and the coldest known sub-stellar brown dwarf (-13 °C). It emits almost exclusively in the thermal mid-infrared (D5 & D6, 3.8-5.0 μm) and is completely invisible in optical and near-IR bands!

Key Science: Switch between D1 and D5/D6 to see the object appear. Use the Motion Ruler to measure its extreme proper motion of 8.1 arcsec/yr.
