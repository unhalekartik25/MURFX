WISE 0855: Sub-stellar Brown Dwarf

WISE 0855 is the fourth-closest known system to the Sun and the coldest known sub-stellar brown dwarf (-13 °C). It emits almost exclusively in the thermal mid-infrared (D5 & D6, 3.8-5.0 μm) and is completely invisible in optical and near-IR bands!

Key Science: Switch between D1 and D5/D6 to see the object appear. Use the Motion Ruler to measure its extreme proper motion of 8.1 arcsec/yr.
