WISE 0855: Sub-stellar Brown Dwarf

WHAT IT IS

WISE 0855 is the fourth-closest known system to the Sun and the coldest known sub-stellar brown dwarf (~250 K, model range 225-260 K). It emits almost exclusively in the thermal mid-infrared (D5 & D6, 3.8-5.0 μm) and is undetectable by SPHEREx in D1-D3.

WHY IT MATTERS

Across 10 months (12 frames), WISE 0855 shifts ~7" (~1.1 SPHEREx pixels at 6.15"/px)—requiring sub-pixel centroiding rather than simple blink comparison.

WHAT TO TRY

Toggle between D1 and D5 to see the brown dwarf materialize. Click Centroid Lock or the Motion Ruler to measure its extreme proper motion of 8.1 "/yr.
